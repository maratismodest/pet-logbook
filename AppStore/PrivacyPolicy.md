# Pet Logbook Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 30, 2026_

Pet Logbook is designed to keep your information private.

## Data we collect

None. Pet Logbook doesn't collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to third parties. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## Data stored on your device

The pets and diary entries you create are stored only on your device. They're removed when you delete them in the app or delete the app.

## Apple Intelligence features

Quick Entry and Vet Summary use Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework. Your text is processed on your device and isn't sent to us.

## Sharing

A Vet Summary PDF is shared only when you choose to share it, using the system share sheet, with the recipient you select.

## Children

Pet Logbook doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
