# Pet Logbook Support

Pet Logbook is a pet health journal for iPhone and iPad. Log feedings, walks, symptoms, medications, and vaccinations, and share a Vet Summary as a PDF before a vet visit.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your device. Pet Logbook has no accounts and no server. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).

**Why are Quick Entry and the written overview unavailable?**
They use Apple Intelligence. Make sure your device supports Apple Intelligence, it's turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri, and the model has finished downloading. Everything else in the app works without it.

**How do I share a summary with my vet?**
Open a pet, tap the … menu → Vet Summary, choose a period, and tap Share PDF.

**How do I delete a pet or an entry?**
Swipe left on it in the list. Deleting a pet also deletes its entries.

**Is Pet Logbook medical advice?**
No. Pet Logbook helps you keep notes. Always consult a veterinarian about your pet's health.
